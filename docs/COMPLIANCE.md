# Compliance Declaration

TSF is designed to minimize regulatory risk and enable compliant market entry through licensed partners.

* Loan servicing, trust management, and tokenization are intended to be handled by regulated entities
* Buyer credit assessments prioritize privacy through soft-pull approaches and encryption
* Credit information is displayed only as non-identifiable scores or ranges
* No sensitive documents or raw credit data are stored on-chain
* Blockchain records contain only cryptographic hashes and Merkle proofs for integrity verification

This submission represents a technical prototype for demonstration purposes only and does not constitute a live offering or solicitation.
